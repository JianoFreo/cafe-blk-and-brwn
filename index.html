Who we are

Café BLK & BRWN started in Barangay Capipisa, Tanza, in May 2021. What began as one small coffee shop has grown into a café and restaurant across Cavite and Metro Manila, serving espresso, chai and chocolate drinks, Freskaró juices and hearty food. The idea stays simple: good coffee, good food, and room to connect.
