Reserve a table

Your information

A little care for your data.

What we ask for when you reserve a table or send an inquiry, and why.

Reservation information

When you send a reservation request we ask for your name, email, mobile number, preferred branch, date, time, number of guests, and an optional note. Inquiries include your name, email and message. Please do not include payment details or other sensitive information in notes.

Why we need it

These details let café staff review your request, prepare for your visit, and contact you to confirm or reschedule. Branch staff can see their own branch’s reservations; administrators can see reservations across branches. Reports summarize reservation counts and guest numbers, not revenue.

Your reservation status

Keep your reservation reference safe. Anyone with your reference can see limited booking details on the reservation status page. Confirmations are sent by email or phone by café staff.

Questions and record requests

To correct or delete your information, contact cafeblkandbrwn@gmail.com.
