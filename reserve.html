Reservation guidelines

  • Requests are confirmed by the branch within a few hours during business hours.
  • Walk-ins are always welcome — a reservation just holds your table.
  • Groups of 8 or more, please call the branch directly.
  • Kindly cancel or reschedule at least 2 hours before your slot.

What happens next

  1. You send your requestYour details reach the café.
  2. Staff check availabilityWe review your branch, date and group size.
  3. You get a confirmationBy email or phone, with your reference.
  4. Enjoy your visitYour corner is ready when you arrive.
